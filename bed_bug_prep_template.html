Heat Treatment Preparation Requirements

Follow these critical instructions to ensure 100% extermination success & home safety.

18-Step Preparation Checklist

1. Sprinklers: Shut down sprinkler system, cap sprinklers, or set system to activate at 200°F+.
2. Alarms: Deactivate or remove smoke, heat, and fire detectors in service areas.
3. Full Access: Unlock all interior areas, storage chests, closets, and cabinets.
4. Electronics: Unplug all electronic devices and appliances (TVs, computers, clocks, etc.).
5. Drawers & Luggage: Open all luggage and drawers. Remove excess clothing and hang.
6. Pets & Plants: Remove ALL pets (including fish in tanks) and live house plants.
7. Vacuum Floors: Floors must be clean & vacuumed; clear space under beds and furniture.
8. Bedding: DO NOT remove sheets, linens, covers, or encasements from mattress.
9. Medications: Remove all prescription medications from premises or store in refrigerator.
10. Trash & Ash: Empty and remove all trash cans, waste bins, and ash trays.
11. Blinds: Pull plastic blinds up completely or take down (heat can cause warping).
12. Valuables & Privacy: Technicians inspect all drawers/closets; remove any private items.
13. Clothing Stacks: Do NOT pile or bag clothing, furniture, or personal belongings tightly.
14. Closet Spacing: Space out hanging clothing in closets to allow heat airflow.
15. Compressed Gases: REMOVE ALL aerosol cans, oxygen tanks, spray paint, propane, etc.
16. Waterbeds: Waterbeds must be completely drained of all water.
17. Air Mattresses: Deflate air mattresses by approximately 5%.
18. Loose Papers: Secure loose papers, photos, and items susceptible to high airflow.

Temperature-Sensitive Items (Protect or Remove)

The following items must be inspected for pests and protected from heat. Items may be placed inside an un-plugged refrigerator, chilled cooler, or removed from the premises entirely during treatment:

• Chocolates, candy & melting foods
• Carbonated & alcoholic beverages
• Medical equipment & devices
• Aerosol / pressurized cans
• Candles, cosmetics, wax, lip balm
• Musical instruments & vinyl records
• Wines & heat-perishable goods
• Artificial Christmas trees
• Fuel, paint thinners & combustibles

Rule of Thumb: Any item that would melt or spoil if left on a closed car dashboard in hot summer weather must be protected or removed.
